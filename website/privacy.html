Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information FRACTALSCREEN (“we”, “us”) collects when you use fractalscreen.com, the Web Player and the Windows app, and how we use it. We collect as little as we can, and we never sell your information. Questions: contact@fractalscreen.com.

What we collect, and why

When you buy a plan

Payments are handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details, name, email address and billing country, and shares with us your email, name, country and subscription details (plan, status and renewal date). We never receive your full card number. We use this to provide your plan, send receipts and handle support. See Stripe’s privacy policy.

License checks

Your license key is your Stripe subscription ID. The Premium Web Player and the Windows Premium app send this key to fractalscreen.com about twice a day to check that your plan is active. Like any web request, this includes your IP address, which our hosting provider keeps briefly in server logs.

When you contact us

The contact form sends us your name, email address and message by email. We use them only to reply, and keep the email as long as needed to help you.

On your own device

Your settings (fractal, colors, speed, music), the start date of your free trial and, for Premium, your license key are saved on your own device: in your browser’s local storage for the Web Player, and in the Windows registry for the Windows app. They aren’t sent to us, except the license key for the check described above.

Analytics and cookies

If you click Accept on our cookie banner, we use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they use. Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_*) and collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser. We don’t use it for advertising. If you click Decline, or don’t choose, Analytics cookies aren’t set. You can change your choice anytime with Cookie settings. See Google’s privacy policy.

We also store your cookie choice in your browser’s local storage, so we don’t ask again.

Website hosting

Our website is hosted by Hostinger, which keeps standard server logs (IP address, pages requested, time and browser) for security and troubleshooting.

Who we share it with

Only the services above: Stripe (payments), Google (analytics, if you accept) and our hosting provider. They process information on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own policies. Some of them are based in the United States or other countries, so your information may be processed outside your own country.

How long we keep it

Purchase records are kept as long as required for tax and accounting. Contact emails are kept as long as needed to help you. Analytics data is kept by Google for 14 months.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see, correct, export or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email contact@fractalscreen.com and we’ll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

FRACTALSCREEN isn’t aimed at children under 16, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above.
