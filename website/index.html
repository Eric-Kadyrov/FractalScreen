Questions

Does it work on iPhone and iPad?

Apple doesn’t allow apps to replace the iPhone or iPad screensaver, so FRACTALSCREEN will run there as an ambient display app. Open it on a stand or bedside and it plays fractals and music with a sleep timer.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is the installer safe?

Windows shows this message for new apps it hasn’t seen many times yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer only copies the screensaver into your user folder and switches it on.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Settings, then Apps, find FRACTALSCREEN and choose Uninstall. Your previous screensaver setting is cleared.

Should I use the desktop app or the Web Player?

Use the desktop app on Windows: it’s a real screensaver and starts by itself whenever your computer is idle. The Web Player works on any device without installing anything, but it only starts while its page is open, so keep it in a browser window or install it as an app.

What happens after the 2-week Windows trial?

The Windows app stops showing fractals and asks you to upgrade to Premium. Upgrade and they come straight back, with all five fractals and every color scheme. The Web Player stays free forever with 3 fractals.

Where do I find my license key?

Right after checkout, your download page shows your key and copies it for you, and the Windows installer fills it in. You can also enter it later in FRACTALSCREEN settings from the Start menu.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Choose Manage subscription at the bottom of this page, sign in with the email you paid with, and cancel in a few clicks. Premium keeps running until the end of the period you’ve paid for, then stops.

How do I make the music louder or quieter?

Use your computer’s or device’s volume, like for any other sound. On Windows you can also give FRACTALSCREEN its own level in the volume mixer. To turn the music off, untick it in FRACTALSCREEN settings.

Where does the music come from?

It’s original music generated live by FRACTALSCREEN, so it never loops and is safe to play in shops and offices on the Business plan.

Will it slow down my computer?

It only runs while your screen is idle and stops the moment you move the mouse or press a key.

Can I use it on screens in my business?

Personal plans are for your own devices. For lobbies, cafés, offices or waiting rooms, choose the Business plan.
