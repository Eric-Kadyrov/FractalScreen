Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

We want you to be happy with FRACTALSCREEN. That’s why it’s free for 4 weeks, with no card needed, so you can try it on your own screens before you pay.

New subscriptions: 14-day money-back guarantee

If you’re not happy with Premium or Business, email us within 14 days of your first payment and we’ll refund it in full. No questions asked.

Renewals

  • Yearly plans: if a yearly renewal catches you by surprise, email us within 14 days of the renewal charge for a full refund.
  • Monthly plans: renewals aren’t refunded, but you can cancel anytime and keep your plan until the end of the month you’ve paid for.

Something not working?

If FRACTALSCREEN doesn’t work on your device and we can’t fix it, we’ll refund the current period in full.

How to ask for a refund

Email contact@fractalscreen.com from the email address you paid with, or use the contact form. Refunds go back to your original payment method and usually arrive within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. A refunded plan ends straight away.

Cancelling

To stop future payments, use Manage subscription at the bottom of fractalscreen.com. See our Terms of Service for details.
